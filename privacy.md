# WD Core Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

WD Core ("the bot", "we") is a Discord bot that translates messages. This policy explains what data the bot handles and why.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data**
- Your Discord user ID and username.
- The date you first used the bot.

**Settings and usage**
- Your chosen languages and plan (Free, Trial or Premium).
- Translation counts per day, used to apply daily limits.

**Message content**
- When you ask the bot to translate a message (emoji, /t, or Apps > Translate), the text of that message is processed. Translated texts may be stored in a translation library so the same text does not need to be translated again.
- The bot does not read or store messages you did not ask it to translate, except for the temporary handling needed to add the translate emoji.

**Support and messages**
- Reports, language suggestions, support tickets, and conversations you send to the developer, including their text and time.
- Messages the developer or support staff send you, and which staff member sent or closed them.
- Server owner tickets, which also include the server name and ID.

**Payments**
- When you buy Premium, we store the transaction ID (if you have one), the name and email you enter, the invoice or receipt number, when you say you paid, the plan, the price, and the request status.
- We also keep the decision on your request (approved, rejected or refunded), who reviewed it and when, any correction made to your details, and private internal notes written by the operator.
- We keep the Premium codes you redeem and your Premium history (grants, redemptions and refunds).
- Payments are made on PayPal. We never receive or store your card number or PayPal password.

**Servers, credit and events**
- When the bot is in a server, we store the server ID and name, the bot settings chosen by the server's admins (such as channels and roles), and the server's Premium credit.
- When a server runs a giveaway or event with the bot, we store its settings, the people who join, and the winners. Winners may receive a direct message from the bot.

## 2. How we use data
- To translate messages and apply your language settings.
- To enforce daily limits and manage Premium access.
- To verify payments, prevent duplicate use of a payment, and handle refunds.
- To answer reports and support requests.
- To run server credit, giveaways and events.
- To keep a record of which staff member handled each request.
- To prevent abuse of the bot.

## 3. Translation providers
To translate text, the text you submit is sent to third-party translation services, which may include Groq, Google (Gemini), Mistral, OpenRouter and DeepL. These services process the text under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit sensitive personal information for translation.

## 4. Storage and security
Data is stored in a cloud database and on the operator's devices. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

## 5. Sharing
We do not sell your data. We share it only with the providers above to perform translations, and with PayPal for payments, or if required by law.

Support, messages and payment requests may be handled by staff the operator trusts. Staff can open only the sections the operator gives them (for example support, purchases, users or inbox), and their actions are recorded with their Discord ID. Administrators of a server can see who joined the giveaways they run.

## 6. Retention
- Account, settings and usage data are kept while you use the bot.
- Payment records are kept for accounting and to prevent duplicate use of a payment.
- Translation library entries and conversations may be kept until you ask for deletion.
- Server credit and event records may be kept until the server or you ask for deletion.

## 7. Your choices and deletion
You can ask to see or delete your data at any time. Use /report in Discord or open the support server: https://discord.gg/8XPHezcAMk. Payment records may be kept where needed for accounting or fraud prevention.

## 8. Children
WD Core is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by Discord in your country). If you believe a child has used the bot, contact us to remove the data.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
